# Governance

TrustCurve was started by @brij. @trustcurve is the initial repository administrator. No independent review board or accreditation body is established.

## Review responsibilities

Contributors propose changes. Reviewers check source relevance, dates, scope, and attribution. Maintainers merge accepted changes and resolve procedural questions. A contributor must not approve their own claim or review an organization with which they have a material conflict.

Require at least one independent approval for substantive record changes. Require two reviewers for contested funding or political claims. If reviewers are unavailable, leave the claim pending rather than presenting it as verified.

Record review decisions in the pull request. A maintainer changes a claim to `reviewed` only after the required review. This means the claim's source and wording were checked; it does not certify safety or independence.

Funding, sponsorship, and self-submission do not buy placement or favorable treatment. Prospective maintainers should propose their scope and disclose conflicts in an issue. Existing maintainers decide openly in that issue.

## GitHub settings to enable

Protect `main`; require pull requests, the `validate` check, and code-owner review. Dismiss stale approvals when a pull request changes. Restrict bypasses. CODEOWNERS alone does not enforce approval. These settings are not enabled by committing this file.

Discussions can be enabled for introductions and methodology proposals. Use issues and pull requests for record changes.
