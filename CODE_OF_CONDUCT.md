# Code of conduct

Be respectful and specific. Critique claims and methods, not personal characteristics. Do not harass contributors, publish private information, or make unsupported accusations.

Disclose relevant relationships. Welcome corrections and distinguish disagreement from misconduct. Maintainers may hide abusive content or limit participation, with an explanation when appropriate.

Report sensitive conduct concerns privately to [@trustcurve on X](https://x.com/trustcurve). Do not put private allegations in a public issue.
