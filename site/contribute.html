PROPOSED CONTRIBUTION PROCESS

Open contributions.
Accountable review.

Community input should improve the evidence. It should not purchase influence or grant a safety endorsement.

  1. Document the source

    Use public primary sources. Separate the source’s claims from your own observations.

  2. Disclose your relationship

    State employment, funding, or commercial ties. Contributors should not review their own organizations.

  3. Review before publication

    A separate reviewer should check the scope and evidence. Suggestions must not change badges or scores automatically.

  4. Credit the work

    Offer optional contributor credit and record accepted changes in a public history. Both remain planned features.
